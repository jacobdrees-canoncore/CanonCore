# Does "record less, only what CanonCore needs" make sense?

**Yes.** Design teams study targeted flows and one instance of each component, not whole apps. They record video only for motion, and they turn what they record into duration, easing and spring values. For the web prototype's 24 blocks, untitled and Apple Music need no new capture: their looks come from code, CSS and measurements already held. Brink needs a handful of stills and four transitions (one of them optional) recorded on the phone. That is one session of about 45 minutes, and none on the Mac. Walking and recording every screen of Brink serves Brink's own spec (CC-33). It does not serve CC-38.

## Part 1: how teams study reference apps today

### Whole apps or targeted flows and components

- **Mobbin is organised by screens, flows and patterns, not by apps.** It offers "Individual mobile and web screens", "End-to-end flows such as onboarding, checkout, account setup, and subscription" and "UI patterns and elements such as dialogs, navigation, forms, tables, empty states, and paywalls". ([mobbin.com](https://mobbin.com/), last updated 5 Nov 2026)
- **Page Flows records one journey at a time.** It offers "actual screen recordings of full user journeys" ([Page Flows FAQ](https://pageflows.com/faq/), undated) and "detailed screen recordings with step-by-step annotations highlighting key UI/UX design elements & interactions" ([pageflows.com](https://pageflows.com/), undated, © 2026).
- **Atomic Design's interface inventory captures one of each pattern.** The method is "capturing one instance of each unique UI pattern", sorted into categories. ([Atomic Design, chapter 4](https://atomicdesign.bradfrost.com/chapter-4/), © 2016; [Conducting an Interface Inventory](https://bradfrost.com/blog/post/conducting-an-interface-inventory/), 11 May 2015)
- **NN/g scopes competitive reviews to key areas and tasks.** "They can dive into a particular feature or area, or encompass an entire site", but "In any broad evaluation, it is best if some key areas are identified to focus the review", and "Focus on representative and key tasks that can be completed across sites." A typical review covers 2 to 4 competitors. ([Competitive Usability Evaluations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/competitive-usability-evaluations/), 5 Jan 2024)
- **Design-system audits gather states and variants as stills.** Figma's advice: "Gather examples of your UI across different platforms and devices, including interactive states and alternate versions", and "Screenshots are your best friend here!" ([Design systems 102](https://www.figma.com/blog/design-systems-102-how-to-build-your-design-system/), 8 Apr 2024). Spotify's audit is per component and per platform: each representative "conducts an audit and diagrams out what's unique to their surface" ([How Spotify's design system goes beyond platforms](https://designsystems.com/how-spotifys-design-system-goes-beyond-platforms/), undated, citing a talk of 20 Sep 2023).
- **Shopify's competitive analysis uses stills, with recordings as an option for tricky parts.** "Use screenshots or brief notes to capture any problem areas", and "If helpful, record your screen using a tool like Loom so you can replay tricky parts." ([Competitive UX analysis](https://www.shopify.com/blog/competitive-analysis-ux), 3 Feb 2026)

No source fetched recommends recording a whole app end to end. Full coverage appears only when a team audits its own product, and even then it is split into component categories.

### When teams capture motion rather than stills

- **Only for what moves.** Atomic Design's inventory treats animation as its own category: "This requires using screen recording software such as QuickTime ... to capture any UI element that moves, fades, shakes, transitions, or shimmies across the screen." ([chapter 4](https://atomicdesign.bradfrost.com/chapter-4/))
- **Mobbin added video only where motion matters.** It introduced "videos to flows" on 21 Mar 2024. On 12 Nov 2025: "Screens with interesting motion are now captured as short videos instead of static images." ([Mobbin changelog](https://mobbin.com/changelog.md))
- **What a system ships is values, not video.** Material's Android motion is "Easing (aka interpolator) and duration theme attributes", plus springs "in three speeds", for example `motionDurationLong2` at 450 ms ([Material Components Android, Motion.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/material-components/material-components-android/master/docs/theming/Motion.md), undated). IBM Carbon has "six static value tokens", for example duration-fast-01 at 70 ms ([Carbon motion](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/motion/overview/), last updated 12 Aug 2026). Atlassian's easing tokens are values, for example `motion.easing.out.practical` at cubic-bezier(0.4, 1, 0.6, 1) ([Applying motion](https://atlassian.design/foundations/motion/applying-motion), undated, © 2026). Salesforce writes `--kx-duration-short` at 150 ms, with clips that only illustrate each value ([Lightning Kinetics](http://v1.lightningdesignsystem.com/kinetics/choreography/making-things-move/), last updated 11 Nov 2026).
- **System motion is left to the system.** "The system already provides subtle animations for interactions with standard interface elements." ([HIG: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion), latest change 9 Sep 2025) Apple publishes no durations for that motion, so only an app's custom motion is worth recording.

**What the practice says for CanonCore:** take stills of the components and states a block needs, read motion values from code where code states them, and record only the transitions that code does not state.

## Recommendation

Record less. Stop walking Brink for CC-38's sake and capture only this, on the phone:

1. **Stills on 1.2.47, light and dark:** the Search tab (empty, typing, results), Library with Folders on and its display menu, and one frame of the video player if the Owner wants Brink's look on video. That is about 6 to 8 captures with their trees, using the walk tooling as it stands.
2. **Recordings**, each once (the Owner, 16 Nov 2026), in light and dark where Brink's look changes; Brink's players "keep one look", so the player clips need only one appearance. The player and episode clips are system motion, recorded because a web block must imitate it:
   - the Home swipe, slow and fast (Shelf)
   - open and close the full player (Player bar)
   - the mini-player minimising on scroll (Player bar)
   - tap into an episode or show (Item hero, optional)
3. **Turn them into values, not cards for every screen.** Measure only the open rows each recording answers: `colorTransitionDuration`, the system sheet and zoom timing, and the minimise. Write them beside `values-from-code.md` as measured. Every other Brink value stays as code states it.

**Estimate:** one phone session of about 45 minutes. Setup and restore take about 10 minutes, going by sessions 2 and 3 (WebDriverAgent, volume, Do Not Disturb, Brink's own Light & Dark switch). Stills take about 10 minutes. The four transitions (one optional) take about 20 minutes, each recorded once and in both appearances where Brink's look changes, which is about 6 clips of 5 to 10 seconds each. If the Owner needs the phone back, split it into two sessions of about 25 minutes: stills, then recordings. **The Mac needs no session.** Brink is iPhone-only in scope. untitled's desktop look comes from the web replica's CSS. untitled Mac's motion comes from CC-391, accepted as read (the Owner, 16 Nov 2026): the lean path retires CC-36's screen tickets, so nothing proves it on the Mac.
