## Decisions

### What the design rests on

- **untitled.stream, Apple Music and Brink are design references, and none leads** (ADR 0025,
  the Owner, 2 Nov 2026, superseding ADR 0020's "untitled leads, the folder its signature
  component"; amended 15 Nov 2026, the Owner, adding Brink). Apple Music comes through the Owner's
  replica AppleMusic27, read at commit `70807ab`, and Brink through Brink's look (CC-33); Arc is
  the base the web uses as it ships, and its own look is one more a block may try. CC-38 picks per
  block and per screen at the end of its phase 2, freely combined, among the looks the Owner chose
  for each block (not every block gets every look). Apple Music (music.apple.com, a study row only),
  Plexamp, Spotify, Feishin and Finamp join every block's study. The frosted folder is one take among others. A folder is a presentation that
  any grouping may use, never a domain term.
- **Outside code is used freely, but ships only under a licence the AGPL can carry** (ADR 0019).
  fayazara agreed on 30 Sep to add a licence to the portfolio repo; its code ships in CanonCore
  once a LICENSE file is in that repo. Apple's AML sample licence is accepted like MIT, with
  Apple's notice kept. Apple's code, logos and artwork may ship in CanonCore itself, not only the
  prototypes, under Apple's permission (ADR 0025, amended 2 Nov 2026, the Owner).
- **The web client is Tailwind 4, the shadcn registry and Motion**, with a DESIGN.md holding the
  tokens and `@shadcn/lint` enforcing them (ADR 0021). Arc and ObsidianUI are evaluated as
  registry sources inside the prototype. (Superseded 2 Oct by ADR 0023: the web client uses Arc
  as it ships, shadcn on Radix, DESIGN.md as a mirror of Arc's tokens, no `@shadcn/lint`;
  ObsidianUI and uselayouts are dropped.)
- **Five clients:** the web, iPhone, iPad (with an adaptive layout of its own), Mac and Apple TV
  (ADR 0009). visionOS and watchOS are not clients.
- **The whole brand arrives at public release** (CC-8): name mark, type, palette and mascot.
  Until then the prototype and the product use a neutral face and palette. Nothing drawn by
  untitled, and not its fonts, goes into CanonCore (ADR 0025). (Amended 2 Oct, ADR 0023: on the web the
  type is Arc's, Geist and Inter at 400 and 500, and the neutral face is Arc's tokens. Amended
  15 Nov 2026, the Owner: the neutral face is the product's only; both prototypes try looks, the web
  per block, ADR 0025, and the Apple prototype per screen, from the web's pick, Apple's platform look
  and the looks the Owner adds.)
- **Nothing waits for TMDB.** CC-14 stays open. The Admin's own key stands until a written yes,
  as CC-2 already says.
- **TARDIS Guide is used, as a private install.** Shaun Robinson's thread of 28 Sep (sets, playlists
  and prerequisites added to TARDIS Guide's API, and beta API access given to the Owner's Bronze
  Patron account after an email describing CanonCore) is, in the Owner's reading, TARDIS Guide's
  yes to CanonCore using its data (decided 30 Sep, after the verification). Its API is open only to
  Patrons, so it stays a private install by URL used with each Admin's own Patron key: the Store
  carries no paid Provider (ADR 0002) and CanonCore ships no key (ADR 0003). The snapshot fetches its
  sets, playlists and Prerequisites through its own ticket. The Tardis Fandom Timelines stay the
  first Ordering source for Doctor Who; TARDIS Guide adds sets, guides and playlists beside them.
  `GLOSSARY.md` gains Prerequisite for what it calls prerequisites.
- **The server targets Node 26**, LTS from 28 Oct 2026 (ADR 0016).

## Driving the iPhone

The route is `driving-the-iphone.md`'s first: WebDriverAgent (signed with Jacob's Apple
Developer team) for taps, swipes, typing and the accessibility tree, and `xcrun devicectl` for
screenshots and screen recordings. Its first measurement is a five-second recording checked with
`ffprobe` for frame rate. (That route is the iPhone's. On the Apple TV `devicectl` takes
screenshots but cannot record, so the Apple prototype records the TV from Device Hub's live view
on the Mac; see the Apple TV paragraph below, 15 Nov 2026.) Enable UI Automation is on, and Brink, MD Vinyl and [untitled] are
installed ([untitled] measured in `driving-the-iphone.md`; the other two installed by Jacob in the grill).

During a session the agent (amended 16 Nov 2026, the Owner: for Brink's gap session, for any gap capture the web prototype's studies need, CC-38, and for every app an Apple prototype session, CC-39, opens, on every device: the iPhone, the Mac and the Apple TV; untitled is no longer recorded):

- installs WebDriverAgent once (Jacob approves it on the phone)
- opens only the app being studied (an Apple prototype session also installs and opens the
  prototype's own debug build), and in it may sign in, play, queue, download and follow; a
  sign-in code sent to the Owner, by email or text, is asked for through Main and never read by the
  agent
- never buys, posts, shares, uploads or deletes anything, never opens an invite or refer link, and
  leaves other apps and account settings alone, except as below (superseding "never buys, posts,
  follows, shares, uploads, deletes or changes settings; in [untitled] it only views")
- turns the volume to zero before the first play
- may switch Appearance (light or dark) and fake the status bar for a recording, and restores both
  before the session ends; whether `devicectl` can set Appearance on a real device is checked in
  the first phone ticket
- may turn Focus on and set Auto-Lock to Never for the session, and restores both before the
  session ends (amended 4 Nov 2026, the Owner: these were his to set); whether the agent can set
  them on a real device is checked in the first phone ticket, and where it cannot, Main asks Jacob
  to set and restore them
- records every state change in the session's notes, and at the end restores volume, Appearance,
  Focus and Auto-Lock
- never answers a permission prompt with Allow: it declines, or stops and tells Jacob
- keeps raw recordings, stills, accessibility trees and dumps out of git, in the replica's `-data` folder; files
  taken from the original that the rebuild loads (fonts, icons, Rive files, shaders, media) go into the
  replica's git (amended 6 Oct 2026; 15 Nov 2026, the Owner, `GLOSSARY.md`)
- records only a gap: a still of a screen no held source shows, or a short clip of custom motion
  the code does not show plainly; system-drawn motion is not recorded, except a reference's system
  motion a web block must imitate, and each clip is recorded once (the Owner, 16 Nov 2026,
  superseding "records the original twice" and the untitled and Brink build comparisons it carried;
  `docs/research/recording-less-2026-11-16.md`; the folder's comparisons ran on macOS 27.0.1 from
  8 Oct 2026)
- tells Jacob before it starts and when it is done; the phone stays unlocked, on charge, with
  Auto-Lock set to Never and Focus on for the session, and untouched

The same rules bind any session that drives an app on the Owner's Mac, such as an Apple prototype
session (amended 16 Nov 2026, the Owner: untitled's Mac app is no longer driven; 15 Nov 2026): there the Mac's own volume, Appearance and Focus are restored at the end, and since
the Mac has no Auto-Lock to set, the session keeps it awake with `caffeinate` for its length.
