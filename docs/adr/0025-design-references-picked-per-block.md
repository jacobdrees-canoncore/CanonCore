# untitled.stream, Apple Music, Brink and Arc are inputs to one combined design, picked per block and per screen

CanonCore's design draws on four inputs, and none leads: untitled.stream, through its web replica and its app replica (`~/canoncore/untitled-replica` and CC-36, the app replica's values from code since 16 Nov 2026); Apple Music, through the Owner's replica AppleMusic27 (`jacobrees-applemusic/AppleMusic27`, at `~/orca/projects/AppleMusic27`, in its own private organisation and Linear workspace); Brink, through Brink's look (CC-33, `~/canoncore/brink-replica`: its values from Brink's code and the captures held, with Mobbin as an older build's context and cards only for what the web prototype's blocks use; amended 16 Nov 2026); and Arc (corrected 22 Nov 2026 to match the title and the amendment of 21 Nov 2026, below; it said three references, untitled.stream, Apple Music and Brink). Arc (ADR 0023) is also the component foundation: the web uses it as it ships, or forks a component outside Arc's own folder (corrected 22 Nov 2026 to match the amendment of 21 Nov 2026, below; it said "Arc is the base", and "base" now names only the base of the prototype's shared set; forks amended 22 Nov 2026, the Owner, ADR 0023). In the web prototype the references, Arc's look among them, are inputs to one combined CanonCore design, not rival finished looks: the Owner decides which piece comes from which source, and the pieces are built as overrides of Arc's roles, or forks of Arc's components outside Arc's own folder (amended 21 Nov 2026, below; forks amended 22 Nov 2026, the Owner, ADR 0023). ## Considered Options

- untitled leads, the folder as its signature component (ADR 0020, 30 Sep 2026): superseded, as above.
- Both as equal foundations, split by job: rejected on 30 Sep 2026 (ADR 0020), because a fixed split leaves every conflict between them open; a pick per block and per screen does not.
- A fixed rule per block for how Apple Music enters: rejected, so each block's grill asks.
- Brink as a fourth look in the web prototype only, beside two references: rejected on 15 Nov 2026 (the Owner), so that a picked Brink take can become CanonCore's design as an untitled or Apple Music take can.
- Every look tried on every block: rejected on 15 Nov 2026 (the Owner), who chooses the looks per block.
- Three separate shared sets, untitled's, Apple Music's and Brink's, built once by CC-298: rejected on 21 Nov 2026 (the Owner), because the references are inputs to one design, not rival finished looks.

## Consequences

- Ship rules (amended 2 Nov 2026, the Owner: "anything can be lifted, everything, everywhere"): under Apple's permission, Apple's code, logos and artwork may ship in CanonCore itself, not only in the prototypes, and the picks decide what does. Apple's sample code under MIT or AML stays as ADR 0019 accepts it. App Review guideline 5.2.5 is still checked at the Apple prototype (CC-39), because it is App Review's rule, not one Apple's permission lifts: every pick there that takes from Apple (its interface, icons, artwork or advertising theme) carries a one-line 5.2.5 note, and the notes are re-read against that day's guideline text at the Apple prototype's audit and again before CanonCore's first external TestFlight build, in the public release's work (amended 15 Nov 2026, the Owner). CanonCore's own brand at public release (CC-8), its colour and mark, stays CC-8's decision.
- Third-party code inside AppleMusic27 (GPL MeloX meshes, and the code its ADR 0003 lists as unlicensed) is free in the private prototypes and reaches CanonCore only through ADR 0019's licence rule.
- CanonCore is adapted from untitled, never a copy of it. untitled's terms forbid derivative works and the Owner's permission is verbal, so anything drawn by untitled (artwork, icons) stays out. Untitled Sans, Klim's font, reaches CanonCore once the Owner has licensed it from Klim (its web and app licences, klim.co.nz/licences), under ADR 0019's rule; the web prototype's files, untitled-replica's five woff2 styles used under the Owner's permission (CC-132), never do (amended 21 Nov 2026, the Owner; it said untitled's licensed fonts, Klim's Untitled Sans, stay out). Until then CanonCore's web client's type is Arc's, Geist and Inter at weights 400 and 500 (corrected 22 Nov 2026; it said "the web's type", which could be read as the web prototype's); the type verdict at the end of the web prototype's phase 2 (CC-323) becomes that purchase (ADR 0023).
- Brink's look in the web prototype may use Brink's own icons and images, inside that private prototype only, as untitled's look uses untitled's fonts and icons (the Owner, 15 Nov 2026). The same holds in the Apple prototype, inside its private repository only, where Brink is a look the Owner may add to an iPhone screen, Brink shipping on the iPhone alone (the Owner, 15 Nov 2026). Nothing of Brink's (files, strings, names, code) reaches CanonCore: a picked Brink take arrives in CanonCore's own words and values (ADR 0022). One exception (amended 16 Nov 2026, the Owner, at CC-33's spec check): the Owner's own research notes in `docs/research/` may name Brink's types, symbols and shader names and describe `brink-replica`'s tables, as evidence for a decision; no file, string or code of Brink's goes in, and nothing else of Brink's does.
- AppleMusic27 lives outside `~/canoncore/`, an exception ADR 0022 records.
- If the folder is picked: its code reaches the public repo only once its source carries a licence (ADR 0019); the web runs Motion's duration-based springs and the Apple app a port of Motion's spring (`folder.md`); on Apple TV the folder's own fan is its focus signal, with no system lift (CC-60's verdict in `folder.md`), and the first real-remote judgement is the Apple prototype's (CC-39), on the Owner's own Apple TV; the fan is the one recorded exception to Apple's system focus (ADR 0023, Scope, 15 Nov 2026).
