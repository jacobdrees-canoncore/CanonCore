# untitled.stream, Apple Music and Brink are design references, picked per block and per screen

CanonCore's design draws on three references, and none leads: untitled.stream, through its web replica and its app replica (`~/canoncore/untitled-replica` and CC-36); Apple Music, through the Owner's replica AppleMusic27 (`jacobrees-applemusic/AppleMusic27`, at `~/orca/projects/AppleMusic27`, in its own private organisation and Linear workspace); and Brink, through Brink's look (CC-33, `~/canoncore/brink-replica`: its cards and its values from Brink's code). Arc (ADR 0023) is the base: the web uses it as it ships, each reference's look is a set of overrides of Arc's roles, and Arc's own look is one more a block may try. The web prototype (CC-38) picks per block and per screen at the end of its phase 2, and picks combine freely: one block may take untitled's structure, Apple Music's motion, Brink's tint and Arc's type. The Owner chooses which looks each block tries, and not every block gets every look. Apple Music (music.apple.com), Plexamp, Spotify, Feishin and Finamp join every block's competitor study.

(Amended 15 Nov 2026, the Owner, in the web prototype's re-pass. It said two references, untitled.stream and Apple Music, with Arc's look a third contender, and that every block's grill asks about Apple Music. Brink is now a full reference, pickable per block, and a picked Brink take can become CanonCore's design like any other. In the web prototype, untitled's look takes its layout and colour from the web replica, the website's own CSS at tag `cc-15-reference`, and its motion from the app replica's values from untitled's code (CC-391), converted for the web by the take that uses them; Apple Music's look comes from AppleMusic27's web app pinned at commit `70807ab`, and music.apple.com is a competitor in the studies only, never a look's source; Brink's look comes from CC-33's cards and values from code. The Navigation and header block (CC-298) builds the three references' looks once, for every block.) The frosted folder (`docs/design/folder.md`) is one take among the others, not a signature component: a folder stays a presentation any grouping may use, never a domain term. This supersedes ADR 0020 whole (the Owner, 2 Nov 2026), because a fixed lead left every block where Apple Music or Arc does better to an exception, while a pick per block and per screen settles each conflict where it arises.

AppleMusic27's code may be ported into the design prototype as takes, rewritten for its stack, and its SwiftUI half into the Apple prototype (CC-39) on the same rule; whether any of it reaches CanonCore is decided by the picks. Apple has given the Owner its permission, bundling SF fonts included (the Owner, 2 Nov 2026), so Apple-measured values are free in both prototypes, and in CanonCore each pick decides whether a value is Apple's, untitled's, Brink's or Arc's.

## Considered Options

- untitled leads, the folder as its signature component (ADR 0020, 30 Sep 2026): superseded, as above.
- Both as equal foundations, split by job: rejected on 30 Sep 2026 (ADR 0020), because a fixed split leaves every conflict between them open; a pick per block and per screen does not.
- A fixed rule per block for how Apple Music enters: rejected, so each block's grill asks.
- Brink as a fourth look in the web prototype only, beside two references: rejected on 15 Nov 2026 (the Owner), so that a picked Brink take can become CanonCore's design as an untitled or Apple Music take can.
- Every look tried on every block: rejected on 15 Nov 2026 (the Owner), who chooses the looks per block.

## Consequences

- Ship rules (amended 2 Nov 2026, the Owner: "anything can be lifted, everything, everywhere"): under Apple's permission, Apple's code, logos and artwork may ship in CanonCore itself, not only in the prototypes, and the picks decide what does. Apple's sample code under MIT or AML stays as ADR 0019 accepts it. App Review guideline 5.2.5 is still checked at the Apple prototype (CC-39), because it is App Review's rule, not one Apple's permission lifts. CanonCore's own brand at public release (CC-8), its colour and mark, stays CC-8's decision.
- Third-party code inside AppleMusic27 (GPL MeloX meshes, and the code its ADR 0003 lists as unlicensed) is free in the private prototypes and reaches CanonCore only through ADR 0019's licence rule.
- CanonCore is adapted from untitled, never a copy of it. untitled's terms forbid derivative works and the Owner's permission is verbal, so anything drawn by untitled (artwork, icons) and its licensed fonts (Klim's Untitled Sans) stay out. On the web the type is Arc's, Geist and Inter at weights 400 and 500, until the web prototype's type verdict (ADR 0023).
- Brink's look in the web prototype may use Brink's own icons and images, inside that private prototype only, as untitled's look uses untitled's fonts and icons (the Owner, 15 Nov 2026). Nothing of Brink's (files, strings, names, code) reaches CanonCore: a picked Brink take arrives in CanonCore's own words and values (ADR 0022).
- AppleMusic27 lives outside `~/canoncore/`, an exception ADR 0022 records.
- If the folder is picked: its code reaches the public repo only once its source carries a licence (ADR 0019); the web runs Motion's duration-based springs and the Apple app a port of Motion's spring (`folder.md`); on Apple TV the folder's own fan is its focus signal, with no system lift (CC-60's verdict in `folder.md`), and the first real-remote judgement is the Apple prototype's (CC-39).
