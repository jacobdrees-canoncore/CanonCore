# Replicas and the design prototype live outside the public repo

Every replica (`GLOSSARY.md`: untitled.stream's web replica, its app replica for the iPhone and Mac apps (the Mac app added on 3 Oct 2026 by CC-36), and Brink's replica; the MD Vinyl, America.gov and tvOS replicas were dropped on 3 Oct 2026, their specs CC-35, CC-40 and CC-34 canceled) and the design prototype live in their own repositories under `~/canoncore/`, each with a private GitHub remote as a backup, and never in CanonCore's repository. One replica is the exception to `~/canoncore/`: AppleMusic27, the Owner's replica of Apple Music, lives at `~/orca/projects/AppleMusic27` in its own private organisation (`jacobrees-applemusic`) and Linear workspace, and is a design reference by ADR 0025 (the Owner, 2 Nov 2026). This is because CanonCore's repository is public (`gh repo view`, 2026-09-30), so anything pushed to it, a branch included, is published. These works reproduce other people's products, whose terms may forbid derivative works (untitled's do; its makers permitted extraction, amended 6 Oct 2026). The private repositories hold the files taken from the apps and sites that their study loads, among them 2016 Untitled Sans files under Klim's Test Font Licence (the web replica's five woff2 files and the app replica's three OTFs, all committed), a further reason they stay private. (Amended 16 Nov 2026, the Owner: the app replica and Brink's are not built further, `GLOSSARY.md`'s Replica.) They may hold code not yet licensed for the AGPL (ADR 0019), and the prototype's data snapshot carries Providers' artwork fetched with the Owner's personal keys. Only what the prototype decides reaches CanonCore's main branch: DESIGN.md, the verdicts on each spec, and the picked variants described in words and tokens. Where a replica keeps its raw material outside git is said by `GLOSSARY.md`'s Replica, not by this record. (Amended 15 Nov 2026, the Owner: it said "recreation" for what `GLOSSARY.md` now calls a replica, AppleMusic27 included, and that the repositories hold "files taken from the apps and sites" without saying they are the files the rebuilds load; the pointer to `GLOSSARY.md` for raw material is new.)

(Amended 15 Nov 2026, the Owner: there are two prototypes, each in its own repository under `~/canoncore/` with a private remote: the web prototype in `design-prototype`, and the Apple prototype in `apple-prototype`, an Xcode project set up by `setup-orca-linear-project`, with its raw captures in `apple-prototype-data` beside the checkout, outside git.)

## Considered Options

- The prototype on a `prototype/design` branch of CanonCore (D-66 of the first grill): rejected, because on a public repository a pushed branch is a publication.
- Local repositories with no remote, as untitled's web replica began: rejected, because a dead disk would lose weeks of work.
- One shared repository for every replica: rejected in favour of one each, so each keeps its own tooling and none waits on another's.
